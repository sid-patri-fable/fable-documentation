### What to Do if the Extension Freezes or Crashes

Fable's browser extensions can be an incredibly useful tool, enhancing your workflow and productivity. However, like any software, it can occasionally freeze or crash, disrupting your tasks. If you encounter issues with a frozen or unresponsive extension—particularly while using Fable’s Browser Extension—there are several steps you can take to troubleshoot and resolve the problem. Here are three key solutions to ensure your extension runs smoothly:

---

#### 1. Limit Your Recording to 10-12 Steps at a Time

One of the most common causes of extension freezes is overloading it with too many steps during a single recording session. While Fable’s Browser Extension is designed to handle complex tasks, it's best to limit the number of steps you record to around 10-12 in one go. Trying to record too many actions can overwhelm the extension, leading to performance issues or crashes.

To avoid this, break down your recording into smaller, manageable parts. Record a few steps at a time, stop the recording, and then proceed with the next set. This method not only prevents crashes but also ensures that your recordings are more precise and organized.

---

#### 2. Disable Other Browser Extensions or Use a Separate Browser Profile

Sometimes, browser extensions can interfere with one another, causing conflicts that affect performance. If you're experiencing freezing or crashing with Fable’s Browser Extension, it might be due to interactions with other installed extensions. Ad blockers, password managers, and other productivity tools can unintentionally cause slowdowns or unresponsiveness.

To diagnose and solve this issue, try using a clean browser profile with no additional extensions installed. You can easily create a new profile in Chrome, Edge, or any other browser, which will help you isolate the problem and ensure the Fable extension runs without interference. This simple test often resolves extension performance issues and gives you a smoother recording experience.

---

#### 3. Reset the Extension State

If the extension becomes unresponsive despite your best efforts, resetting its state is a quick and effective fix. Fable’s Browser Extension allows you to reset its state easily without needing to uninstall or restart the browser. 

To reset the extension, click the gray square button located in the top-right corner of the extension popup. This action will refresh the extension and clear any minor issues that may have caused it to freeze. By resetting, you can restore the extension to its normal functionality without disrupting your ongoing tasks.

---

### Conclusion

When your browser extension freezes or crashes, it’s essential to troubleshoot the issue promptly to avoid disruptions in your workflow. By keeping your recordings to 10-12 steps, minimizing potential conflicts from other extensions, and resetting the extension state if necessary, you can prevent and resolve most performance issues. Following these simple steps ensures a smoother, more reliable experience with Fable’s Browser Extension.
