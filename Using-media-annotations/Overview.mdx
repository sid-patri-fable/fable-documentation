# Overview of Media Annotations in Fable

Media annotations are a powerful feature in Fable that allow you to enhance your interactive demos by incorporating audio and video content instead of text-based explanations. These annotations provide a more dynamic and engaging way to describe features, helping users understand the context and functionality of a product in a more immersive way.

## What Are Media Annotations?

Unlike traditional text-based annotations, **media annotations** rely on multimedia elements such as audio clips or videos to deliver instructions or explain features within your interactive guides. This allows for a richer, more engaging user experience, as viewers can hear or see demonstrations of key features in action, rather than just reading about them.

## Benefits of Using Media Annotations

Media annotations offer several advantages for creating effective and memorable guides:

- **Enhanced Engagement:** Audio and video content can capture users' attention more effectively than static text, making it easier to hold their interest as they interact with your guide.
  
- **Improved Clarity:** Complex features or workflows can be demonstrated visually or audibly, reducing the chance of misunderstanding.

- **Better Accessibility:** Media annotations can cater to different learning preferences. Users who prefer listening or watching over reading will find these annotations more accessible and easier to follow.

- **Human Connection:** Adding a voice-over or personalized video to a demo makes the guide feel more interactive and engaging, fostering a closer connection with the audience.

By leveraging media annotations, you can make your interactive demos more impactful, accessible, and engaging for all users.
