# Video guides in Fable

Video guides, also known as video annotations, are one of Fable’s most impactful features, designed to take your interactive demos to the next level. Instead of relying solely on text-based explanations, video guides allow you to integrate rich video content directly into your demos. This adds a dynamic and engaging layer to your guides, making it easier for users to grasp complex features and understand the product's context and functionality in a more immersive, visual format.
