### Audio Guides in Fable

Audio guides, or audio annotations, stand out as one of Fable’s most transformative features, designed to elevate your interactive demos significantly. Rather than relying exclusively on text-based explanations, audio guides enable you to embed rich audio content directly into your demos. This enhancement introduces a dynamic and engaging layer, allowing users to experience your content in a more immersive and interactive way.

With audio guides, you can convey complex features and provide detailed context about your product in a manner that's both intuitive and captivating. This feature not only aids in better comprehension but also enriches the overall user experience by integrating voice narration with visual elements, ensuring a more thorough and engaging presentation.

Explore the Fable demo below to see firsthand how seamlessly you can incorporate audio guides into your interactive demos and transform the way your users engage with your product!
