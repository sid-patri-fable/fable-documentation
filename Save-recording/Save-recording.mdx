# Saving a Recorded Demo

Saving your demo in Fable is a simple and streamlined process. Once you've finished recording using the browser extension, Fable guides you through an intuitive wizard to finalize the recording. You'll be prompted to give your demo a name and personalize the annotation card by selecting its color and shape. This allows you to create a professional and polished demo that's tailored to your audience.

The demo below walks you through the entire process, showing how easy it is to save and customize your recorded demo with Fable’s user-friendly interface.
