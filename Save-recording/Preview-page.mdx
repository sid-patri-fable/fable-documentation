# Previewing Your Demo

After saving a new recording in Fable, you’ll have the opportunity to preview your demo before finalizing it. This preview feature allows you to replay the demo and ensures everything looks and flows as expected. Additionally, Fable provides the option to view your demo in various screen sizes, such as small, medium, and large viewports, as well as mobile screens. This allows you to see how your demo will appear across different devices, ensuring a seamless experience for your audience, regardless of the platform they use.

The Fable demo below demonstrates how to preview your demo, replay it, and adjust the viewport settings to ensure it looks perfect on all screen sizes.
