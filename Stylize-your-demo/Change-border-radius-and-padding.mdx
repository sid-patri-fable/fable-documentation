# Customizing Border Radius and Padding in Fable

Fable offers powerful customization options that allow you to fine-tune the appearance of annotation cards, giving you greater control over the visual style of your guides. Two key design elements you can adjust are the border radius and padding of the annotation cards, enabling you to create a refined and polished look that seamlessly aligns with your brand’s aesthetic.
