# Customizing Colors and Fonts in Fable

Fable provides powerful branding tools that allow you to customize the colors and fonts used in your guides, ensuring they align perfectly with your company’s visual identity. These customizations help maintain a consistent brand experience across all touchpoints, whether internal or customer-facing.

The demo below walks you through how to easily change both the colors and fonts in Fable, allowing you to create visually cohesive, branded guides that resonate with your audience.
