# Toggle Watermark

Fable provides you with the flexibility to control whether or not its watermark appears in the guides you create. The watermark can be a valuable asset in certain contexts, such as adding a layer of credibility or showcasing the tool behind the guide. However, there are situations where you may prefer a more polished, brand-exclusive presentation, particularly when creating customer-facing documentation or internal materials designed to reflect your company’s unique identity.

Explore the Fable demo below to learn how to quickly toggle the watermark on or off for your guides, giving you the power to customize every aspect of your presentation.
