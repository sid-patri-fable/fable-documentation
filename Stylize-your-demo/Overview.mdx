# Overview

Fable’s branding features empower you to create a personalized and cohesive experience for your audience by tailoring the visual elements of your guides and demos to match your brand. With Fable, you can easily customize various design elements to ensure consistency across your guides and enhance user engagement.

## Why Customize Branding?

Customizing the branding of your Fable guides allows you to:
- **Strengthen brand identity** by incorporating your company’s logo, colors, and style.
- **Maintain consistency** across internal and external communications.
- **Enhance the user experience** by presenting guides that align with your brand's aesthetic.

## Customizable Branding Elements

Fable provides several options to customize branding, allowing you to control everything from color schemes to visual effects.

### 1. **Customize Colors**
   Fable allows you to set your brand’s primary and secondary colors, ensuring that the guides match your company’s color palette. These colors will be applied to key elements like buttons, text highlights, and borders, creating a cohesive visual experience.

### 2. **Typography and Fonts**
   You can customize the typography to align with your brand’s style by selecting from a variety of font families available within Fable. This ensures that your guides reflect the same font as your brand's website or other marketing materials.

### 3. **Border Radius and Padding of Annotation Cards**
   Fable gives you control over the design of annotation cards, including the border radius and padding. This feature allows you to adjust the curvature of the card corners and the spacing around the text or content inside the card, creating a more polished look that matches your brand’s visual language.

### 4. **Customize Selection Shape and Selection Effect**
   Fable enables you to customize the visual cues used to highlight selected elements in your guides. You can change both the **shape** of the selection and the **effect** applied to it, ensuring that the selection aligns with your brand's design standards.

### 5. **Toggle Fable’s Watermark**
   Fable allows you to control the visibility of its watermark in the guides you create. While the watermark can add credibility to your guides, you may prefer to disable it in certain cases, such as when creating customer-facing documentation or branded internal resources.
