# Switching organizations in Fable

Organizations allow you to manage different functions across your organization such as sales, marketing, etc. Hence, users of a specific workspace can manage their demos in a containerized environment and have their own global styling and preferences.

The Fable demo below shows how to switch organization in Fable.

<FableEmbed src="https://app.sharefable.com/embed/demo/switch-organizations-mcpz6fp434mtbza8" />

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1
Click on your display picture present in the top left corner of the screen in the header. There you will see the your current organization along with the ability to switch to a different organization.

<img alt="..." src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/a1214a33-2789-42a9-8017-1f363089189c"></img>

### Step 2
Click on switch organization button. This action will take you to a different screen. Here you will find all the organizations for you to switch to as well as the option to create a new one if you want. To change to a different organization, simply select and you are good to go!

<img alt="..." src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/9b6de3c5-25eb-451a-9a29-f7176ae89821"></img>

### Step 3 (Optional)
If you want to create a new organization instead, click on the button to do that. After this action, you will be prompted to give a name for this new organization. Give a name and click "Create new" button!

<img alt="..." src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/e25fc2f8-1e56-49ba-9d85-cf6b6961fc50"></img>

And just like that, you can switch into different organizations in Fable!
