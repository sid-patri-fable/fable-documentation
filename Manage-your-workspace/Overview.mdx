# Workspace Management Overview

Managing workspaces in Fable is straightforward. Workspaces are called Organizations in Fable. Think of an organization as a self-contained environment where you can organize your demos and set global styling preferences. Different teams within an organization, such as sales or marketing, use this feature to manage their specific functions efficiently.
