# Inviting a New User to an Organization

Inviting a user to an organization in Fable is a breeze.

The demo below walks you through the process of inviting a user to an organization.

<FableEmbed src="https://app.sharefable.com/embed/demo/invite-user-4jz2pgylsdifoeo4" />

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1
Navigate to the User Management section in the sidebar.

<img alt="..." src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/6390507c-dfc5-4db6-bf9f-85fe25feb3fd"></img>

### Step 2
Click the "Invite a User" button at the top.

<img alt="..." src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/9f0558c3-4e05-4e27-adce-5a6d9ef695b4"></img>

### Step 3
A modal will appear, prompting you to enter the email address of the user you want to invite. Enter the email and click "Invite."

<img alt="..." src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/c0852a9b-8096-48fb-a488-203d91b5ae32"></img>

### Step 4 
Once you click "Invite," an invitation link will be generated. You can share this link with the user to complete the process.

<img alt="..." src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/d2cde60c-7113-4f67-a227-db431e3929ed"></img>

In just four simple steps, you've successfully added a user to your Fable organization.
