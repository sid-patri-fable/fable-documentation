# Modules Overview

In today’s fast-paced digital environment, capturing and maintaining user attention can be challenging, especially when dealing with lengthy demos. To help you create more engaging and focused presentations, Fable offers a feature called Modules.

Modules allow you to break down long demos into smaller, more manageable segments. This segmentation helps ensure that each part of your demo is concise and to the point, making it easier for your audience to stay engaged. By organizing your content into these bite-sized pieces, you can highlight specific features or aspects of your application without overwhelming the viewer.

Additionally, Modules provide flexibility in how you present your content. You can give each segment a title and description, making it clear what each part of the demo covers. This clarity helps users quickly find the information they’re most interested in. Furthermore, if you want to drive user action, you can add a call-to-action (CTA) to any segment, guiding viewers toward the next steps.

Modules not only make your demos more organized and user-friendly but also empower your audience to interact with the content on their own terms. By giving them the ability to navigate directly to the segments they care about, you enhance their experience and increase the effectiveness of your presentation.
