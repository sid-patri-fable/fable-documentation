Creating a module in an interactive demo with Fable is both simple and efficient. Within the Fable Canvas Editor, there's a dedicated feature designed to streamline the process, allowing you to create modules with just a single click. This user-friendly tool ensures that even those with minimal technical experience can effortlessly add modules to their demos.

The following Fable demo will walk you through the entire process step by step, demonstrating how to leverage this powerful feature to enhance your interactive presentations. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced user, this guide will make module creation quick and intuitive.
