Each module in your interactive demo comes with a title, which is displayed on the button that opens the module popover. This title is a key element of your demo, as it helps users quickly identify the content or feature associated with the module. Customizing the title to fit your specific needs is a simple and straightforward process within the Fable Canvas Editor.

Whether you're looking to make your modules more intuitive, match your branding, or simply update the text, changing the title is both quick and easy. The following Fable demo will guide you through the steps to modify the module title, ensuring your interactive demo is both clear and engaging for your audience.
