# Customizing and Adding a CTA in Module Popovers

Fable gives you the ability to enhance your interactive demo by adding a Call to Action (CTA) within a module popover. This feature is not only easy to implement but also highly customizable, allowing you to tailor the CTA to perfectly fit the needs of your demo.

You can personalize the CTA in various ways, such as changing the text to align with your messaging, adjusting the color and sizing to match your branding, and setting the link to direct users to a specific page or resource. This level of customization ensures that your CTA is both visually appealing and effective in driving user engagement.

Adding a CTA to your module popover is a simple process within the Fable Canvas Editor. The following Fable guide will walk you through the steps to seamlessly integrate and customize a CTA, empowering you to create a more interactive and actionable demo experience.
