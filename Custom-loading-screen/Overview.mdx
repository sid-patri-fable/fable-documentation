# Overview of Custom Loading Screen

When your interactive demos are loading for the first time, Fable displays a loading screen to bridge the gap until they’re ready to be viewed. This loading screen typically includes a logo, optional animation, and loader text. By default, the loading screen features Fable's logo and a standard loader text.

However, Fable offers you the flexibility to customize this loading screen to align with your brand's identity. You can replace the default elements with your own logo, choose an animation that matches your brand's style, and personalize the loader text. This ensures that from the very beginning, your interactive demos feel like a seamless extension of your brand, delivering a consistent and professional experience to your audience.
