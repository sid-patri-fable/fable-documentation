# Changing the Custom Loader Logo

Your logo is a key element of your brand identity, and Fable understands its importance in maintaining a consistent brand presence. That’s why Fable allows you to easily customize the logo displayed on the loading screen before your demo begins. This feature ensures that every aspect of your interactive demos, right from the start, aligns with your brand’s visual identity.

The demo embedded below provides a detailed walkthrough on how to change the logo in the loader. Follow the steps to seamlessly upload and apply your custom logo, enhancing the branding of your demos and delivering a more cohesive experience to your audience.
