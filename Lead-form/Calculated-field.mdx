## Calculated Fields: A Powerful Tool for Lead Forms

**What are Calculated Fields?**

Calculated fields are invisible fields in lead forms that automatically populate with specific values. These values can be determined either through URL query parameters or directly within the lead form itself. By using calculated fields, you can streamline data collection and improve lead qualification processes.

**Common Use Cases for Calculated Fields:**

* **Tracking Source:** Automatically populate a hidden field with the source of the lead (e.g., Google Ads, social media, email marketing) to analyze marketing campaign effectiveness.
* **Passing Unique Identifiers:** Transmit unique identifiers (e.g., UTM parameters, session IDs) to track lead journeys and attribute conversions accurately.
* **Pre-filling Form Data:** Reduce form abandonment by pre-filling fields with information from external sources (e.g., CRM, marketing automation platform), saving users time and effort.
* **Enhancing Lead Scoring:** Assign calculated scores based on specific criteria (e.g., job title, company size, geographic location) to prioritize leads for follow-up.
* **Triggering Custom Actions:** Initiate automated workflows or send notifications based on calculated field values (e.g., if a lead meets certain criteria, send a personalized email).

By effectively utilizing calculated fields, you can gather valuable data, automate processes, and improve your lead generation and nurturing efforts.
