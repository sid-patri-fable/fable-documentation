# Setting the Primary Key in Your Lead Form

In every lead form, a primary key is designated to uniquely identify each submission. Typically, the email field is set as the primary key, ensuring that whenever a user fills out the lead form, a new entry is created in your CRM, identified by this key. This primary key is crucial for organizing and tracking your leads effectively.

With Fable, you have the flexibility to assign the primary key attribute to any field of your choice, depending on your specific needs. Whether you prefer to use the email field or another piece of information as the primary key, Fable makes it easy to configure.

The process of setting the primary key is simple and intuitive, allowing you to customize your lead forms with ease.
