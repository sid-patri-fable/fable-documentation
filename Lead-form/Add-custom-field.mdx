# Adding Custom Fields to Your Lead Form

Fable provides a range of predefined fields that can be effortlessly added to your lead forms, allowing you to collect essential information from your audience. In addition to these standard fields, Fable also gives you the flexibility to create and add custom fields, enabling you to capture the specific data that matters most to your business. For instance, if you need to gather information about a company’s revenue, you can easily add a custom field for that purpose.

The process of adding a custom field is straightforward and user-friendly. With just a few clicks, you can tailor your lead form to meet your unique requirements, ensuring that you capture the exact information you need.

To see how it's done, check out the Fable demo below, which guides you through the simple steps of adding a custom field to your lead form.
