# Rearranging Field Order in Your Lead Form

After adding multiple fields like First Name, Email, and Company to your lead form, you may want to adjust the order in which they appear to better suit your needs. Fable makes this process simple and intuitive. With just a few clicks, you can rearrange the fields in your lead form directly within Fable's Rich Text Editor.

To change the order, simply drag and drop the fields to your desired position. This flexibility allows you to customize the layout of your lead form, ensuring that the most important information is presented in the optimal sequence.
