# Adding New Fields to Your Lead Form

Fable offers a variety of predefined fields that can be seamlessly added to your lead forms, enabling you to gather essential information from your audience. The available fields include:

- Email
- First Name
- Last Name
- Country
- Industry
- Mobile
- Website

Incorporating these fields into your lead form is incredibly simple. All it takes is a click on the "Add New Field" button, followed by selecting the desired field from the list. Once added, Fable automatically handles the synchronization of data with your CRM, ensuring that the information is accurately mapped to the appropriate columns.

This effortless process allows you to customize your lead forms to meet your specific needs, enhancing your ability to capture and organize vital data efficiently.
