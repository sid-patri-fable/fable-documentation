# Integrating a Lead Form

Integrating a lead form into your Fable interactive demo is straightforward and efficient. With Fable, you can embed a lead form within any annotation at any step of your demo, seamlessly integrating it into the user experience. The lead form is directly embedded within the annotation text, allowing you to capture valuable information without disrupting the flow of the demo.

To add a lead form, simply click the lead form button in Fable's Rich Text Editor, and it will be inserted into your annotation. This feature empowers you to engage with your audience effectively while guiding them through your demo.
