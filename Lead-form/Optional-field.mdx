# Making Fields Optional in Your Lead Form

In a Fable lead form, all fields are mandatory by default, meaning users must complete every field before they can proceed. However, there may be situations where certain information is not essential, and you want to allow users to skip those fields.

Fable offers the flexibility to mark specific fields as optional, enabling users to leave them blank and continue with the demo. This feature ensures a smoother user experience, particularly when certain details aren't critical to your lead capture process.

Customizing fields in this way is straightforward and can significantly enhance the usability of your lead form.
