# Reselecting Elements for Mobile View

Fable offers a powerful feature that allows you to attach annotations to different elements depending on whether the demo is viewed on mobile or desktop. This means you can customize where your annotations appear for each device type, ensuring that important information is always positioned clearly and effectively. On desktop, an annotation may be placed next to one element, while on mobile, it can be dynamically adjusted to align with another element, preserving the clarity and flow of your demo across various screen sizes.

By tailoring annotations for both desktop and mobile views, Fable helps you maintain the highest level of fidelity in your demos, ensuring your audience experiences a polished and professional presentation regardless of the device they use.

Explore the demo below to see how you can easily reselect elements for annotations in mobile and desktop views, and take full advantage of this feature to create seamless, responsive demos.
