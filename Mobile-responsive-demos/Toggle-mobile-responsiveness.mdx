# Toggling Mobile Responsiveness

Fable gives you full control over the responsiveness of your demos, allowing you to easily toggle mobile responsiveness for each individual demo. When mobile responsiveness is enabled, the demo's user interface automatically adjusts and adapts to various mobile screen sizes, ensuring a smooth and intuitive experience for your viewers. If mobile responsiveness is turned off, Fable scales the UI to fit the device, delivering a more consistent yet standard presentation.

Additionally, Fable allows you to preview how your demo will appear on mobile devices in both portrait and landscape orientations. This feature helps you ensure that your content looks polished and professional, regardless of how your audience views it.

The demo below walks you through the process of toggling mobile responsiveness and switching between portrait and landscape modes to see how your demo will be displayed on different devices.
