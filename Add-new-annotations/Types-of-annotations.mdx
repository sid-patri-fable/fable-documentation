### Types of Annotations in Fable

Fable offers a variety of annotation types to help you effectively guide users through your demos and highlight key features of your app. Each annotation type is designed for different use cases, ensuring you can tailor your annotations to the specific needs of your demo. Here are the three annotation types currently available in Fable:

1. **Guide Annotation**: This type of annotation is directly attached to a specific element on the screen. It acts as a step-by-step guide for users, pointing to individual components or features of your app. Guide annotations are ideal for scenarios where you want to provide contextual instructions or explanations related to a particular part of the interface.

2. **Cover Annotation**: Unlike guide annotations, cover annotations appear in the center of the screen and are not tied to any specific element. These annotations are perfect for providing general information, overviews, or instructions that apply to the entire interface or a large section of the app. They allow you to deliver important messages without being restricted to a single element.

3. **Multi-Annotation (Beta)**: Fable’s multi-annotation feature, currently in beta, allows you to highlight multiple elements on the screen at the same time. This is especially useful for demonstrating complex workflows or showcasing how different parts of the app interact. With multi-annotations, you can draw the user's attention to various components simultaneously, providing a more comprehensive view of your app’s functionality.

By utilizing these different types of annotations, you can create an engaging and informative demo experience tailored to your audience's needs. The Fable demo below illustrates how each type of annotation works and can be used effectively within your app.
