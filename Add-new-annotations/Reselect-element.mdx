### How to Reselect an Element in Fable

Fable offers a convenient feature that allows you to reselect the element to which an annotation is attached. This functionality is especially useful when you've accidentally selected the wrong element or need to make adjustments to your demo after initial setup.

Here's how this feature enhances your workflow:

1. **Correct Mistakes Easily**: If you accidentally click the wrong element when creating an annotation, Fable’s reselect feature allows you to quickly fix it. Rather than starting over, you can simply choose the correct element without disrupting your entire annotation setup.

2. **Fine-Tune Your Demos**: This feature gives you the flexibility to make refinements as you perfect your demo. Whether you want to adjust the position of the annotation or update the element it’s attached to, you can do so effortlessly, ensuring that your demo remains accurate and polished.

3. **Streamlined Workflow**: By enabling you to reselect elements at any point in the annotation process, Fable helps maintain a smooth and efficient workflow. You can correct mistakes in real-time without the hassle of redoing previous steps, saving time and effort.
