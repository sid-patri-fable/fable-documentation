# Add Global Styling to All Your Demos

Fable offers a global styling editor that allows you to manage the visual presentation of all your demos from one central place. This ensures that every demo you create within your workspace maintains a consistent and professional look and feel.

The demo below provides an overview of the global styling editor. Let's explore each option in detail.

<FableEmbed src="https://app.sharefable.com/embed/demo/using-the-global-styli-d8nviek5g1jcm6xy" />

## Common Styling

In the Common Styling section, you can configure the following settings:

1. **Company Logo**
2. **Company URL**
3. **Loading Text**
4. **Font Family for Annotations**
5. **CTA Styling (Size and Primary Color)**

### Changing the Company Logo

To update your company logo, simply click the file upload option and select the logo file. Once uploaded, a preview will appear on the right side for you to confirm.

<img alt="company logo" src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/4fa1c4c1-194c-4b33-8ef6-59aaf3ee7978"></img>

### Changing the Company URL

This URL is associated with your company logo, typically used in places like the header. Configure this link to direct users to your company’s landing page.

<img alt="Changing the company URL" src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/8376f51b-7f02-4250-b201-7e2666b103e5"></img>

### Changing the Demo Loading Text

Before your demo starts, a loader is displayed with default text. You can modify this text to better align with your brand's tone or add something witty for your audience to read while waiting for the demo to load.

### Changing the Font Family

Select a font family for your annotation text from a variety of options using this dropdown selector.

### Changing the CTA Styling

Customize the look of your Call-to-Actions (CTAs), such as "Next" or "Back" buttons, by adjusting their size and primary color. Changes are instantly previewed on the right side.

## Annotation Styling

The Annotation Styling section allows you to adjust various aspects of your annotations, including:

1. **Annotation Colors**
2. **Border Radius and Padding**
3. **Selection Properties: Shape, Effect, and Color**
4. **Next and Back Button Types**
5. **Toggle Watermark and Progress Bar**

Let’s dive into each setting:

### Body Background Color

Set the background color of your annotation card, usually matching it with your brand’s primary color to create visually striking demos.

### Body Border Color

By default, this is set to transparent, but you can add a border to your annotation cards if your brand’s design system includes it.

### Font Color

The font color is automatically set based on the contrast with the background color. However, if your brand uses specific colors, you can adjust this setting.

### Container Border Radius

Adjust the roundness of your annotation cards to match the button styles in your app. Larger companies often prefer sharper corners, while smaller companies might go for a more rounded look.

### Container Padding 

Set the vertical and horizontal padding inside the annotation card by entering two numeric values separated by a space. This changes the padding around the content within the card.

### Selection Color

This color is applied to the mask around the selected element. Match it with your annotation background color or use your brand’s accent color.

### Selection Shape

Fable offers two selection shapes:
1. **Box:** Draws a box around the selected element, ideal for highlighting small areas like buttons.
2. **Pulse:** Displays a pulsating circle, perfect for drawing attention to larger areas like forms.

### Selection Effect

For box-shaped selections, choose between:
1. **Regular:** A static box.
2. **Blinking:** A box with an alternating border thickness, useful for hotspot elements that prompt user interaction.

### Progress Bar

Toggle the progress bar at the top of the demo, which indicates how much of the demo is completed.

### Watermark

Fable displays two watermarks in your demo:
1. A small Fable logo on the annotation card.
2. A Fable logo at the bottom left or right of the screen.

You can toggle the visibility of these watermarks on or off.

### Button Properties

Customize the CTA buttons on annotation cards:
1. **Button Text:** The text displayed on the button.
2. **Button Type:** Choose between Primary (solid background), Outline (transparent background with a border), or Link (no border or background).

By default, the "Next" button is set to Primary, and the "Back" button is set to Link, but these can be easily modified.
