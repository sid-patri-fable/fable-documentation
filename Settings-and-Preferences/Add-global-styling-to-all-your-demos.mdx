# Add global styling to all your demos

Fable comes with a global styling editor, which allows you to manage every single aspect of styling your demos in one place. Hence, no matter how many demos you create in your workspace, every single demo with inherit a uniform look and feel, hence, giving a professional vibe.

The Fable demo below gives an overview of the global styling editor, let's take a deep dive into each option and understand what it does.

<FableEmbed src="https://app.sharefable.com/embed/demo/using-the-global-styli-d8nviek5g1jcm6xy" />

## Common Styling

The common styling section includes options to configure or modify the following settings:
1. Company logo
2. Company URL
3. Loading text shown before demo
4. Font family of annotations
5. CTA styling such as their size and primary color.
