# Overview of Settings and Preferences

The Fable settings and preferences offer two powerful customization options:

- **Global Styling:** Easily modify the styling across all your demos within your workspace for a consistent look and feel.
- **Custom Domain:** Host all your demos on a domain of your choice to strengthen your brand identity.
