# Qualifications in Demo Hub

Demo Hub goes beyond just presenting demos; it empowers you with robust qualification features designed to create a more personalized and engaging experience for your users. With these tools, you can interactively gauge your users' interests, preferences, and needs, enabling you to showcase the demos that are most relevant to them.

The qualification builder within Demo Hub provides a comprehensive set of options to customize user interactions. You can incorporate single-select and multi-select questions, gather valuable information through lead forms, and guide users with text steps. By leveraging these features, you can dynamically tailor the demo content to match the specific requirements and expectations of your audience, ensuring that each user receives a highly relevant and impactful experience.
