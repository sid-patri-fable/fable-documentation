# General and CTA Settings in Demo Hub

## General Settings

The General settings in Demo Hub offer essential customization options to align the hub with your brand's identity. Here’s what you can configure:

1. **Company Logo**: Upload your company logo, which will be prominently displayed in the header on the left-hand side, reinforcing your brand presence.
   
2. **Base Font Size**: Set the base font size for the text within the Demo Hub. This value will be inherited by all text elements, ensuring consistent typography throughout your demos.

3. **Font Family**: Choose any valid font from Google Fonts to match your brand's style and improve the overall visual appeal of your Demo Hub.

## CTA Settings

The CTA (Call-to-Action) settings provide flexibility in how you engage with your audience. You can add or remove CTAs, as well as customize each one individually. The customization options include:

1. **Button Text**: Define the text that will appear on the CTA button, making it clear and compelling.
   
2. **Button Type**: Select the style of the button, choosing from Primary, Outline, or Link, depending on the desired emphasis.
   
3. **Button Link**: Set the URL where the button should direct users, guiding them to the next step in their journey.

4. **Button Colors**: Customize the button's color scheme to ensure it aligns with your branding and stands out on the page.

5. **Border Radius**: Adjust the border radius to control the button's shape, whether you prefer sharp edges or a more rounded appearance.

These settings give you the tools to create a Demo Hub that not only looks professional but also drives user engagement effectively.
