# Managing Sections in Demo Hub

A Demo Hub is structured into sections, each dedicated to a specific group of demos that are logically related. Every section includes a title and a description, helping to organize and categorize the content effectively. By creating multiple sections, you can tailor the Demo Hub to showcase different parts of your application, ensuring that each aspect is clearly represented.

The Fable demo below provides a step-by-step guide on how to create these sections, modify their titles and descriptions, and add or remove demos within them, allowing you to customize your Demo Hub to fit your needs.
