# Lead Form Qualification

Lead Form Qualification is a powerful feature within Demo Hub that allows you to seamlessly integrate lead capture into the qualification process. This step is designed to collect valuable user information through a customizable lead form, which can then be synced with your CRM integrations and tracked within Fable's Analytics Dashboard. By incorporating a Lead Form Step, you can not only engage users but also gather crucial data that supports your sales and marketing efforts.

### Adding a Lead Form Step

The Lead Form Step can be inserted at any point in the qualification process, allowing you to capture user details at the most strategic moment. Whether you want to collect information at the beginning of the user journey, midway through, or before showing specific demos, this feature gives you the flexibility to tailor the experience to your needs.

When creating a Lead Form Step, you can customize the form fields to capture the information most relevant to your goals. Common fields might include:
- **Name**
- **Email Address**
- **Company Name**
- **Job Title**
- **Phone Number**
- **Industry**
- **Additional Notes**

These fields are fully customizable, allowing you to gather the precise data you need to qualify leads and tailor follow-up actions.

### Syncing with CRM Integrations

Once users submit the lead form, the collected data is automatically synced with your connected CRM systems. Whether you're using Salesforce, HubSpot, or another CRM platform, this seamless integration ensures that all lead information is captured and stored in one central location. This helps streamline your sales process, making it easier for your team to track, manage, and nurture leads based on the data collected during the qualification process.

### Tracking in Fable's Analytics Dashboard

In addition to CRM syncing, the data from the Lead Form Step is also fed into Fable's Analytics Dashboard. This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of user interactions, including detailed insights into the lead forms that have been submitted. You can monitor key metrics such as submission rates, the most commonly selected form fields, and the overall impact of your lead forms on user engagement and conversion rates.

By analyzing this data, you can refine your lead capture strategy, identify which qualifications are most effective, and optimize the content and placement of your lead forms to improve results.

### Enhancing User Experience and Lead Generation

Incorporating a Lead Form Step into your Demo Hub Qualification process not only enhances user engagement but also provides you with valuable insights into your audience. It allows you to capture high-quality leads while they are actively engaging with your product demos, increasing the likelihood of conversion.

Moreover, by syncing this data with your CRM and analyzing it in Fable's Analytics Dashboard, you can ensure that your marketing and sales teams have the information they need to follow up effectively. This holistic approach to lead generation helps you build stronger relationships with prospects, ultimately driving better outcomes for your business.

### Conclusion

Lead Form Qualification is an essential tool for any Demo Hub looking to capture and convert leads more effectively. By offering a user-friendly way to gather information, integrating seamlessly with CRM systems, and providing actionable insights through analytics, this feature empowers you to create a more impactful and data-driven demo experience. Whether you're focused on generating leads, improving user engagement, or enhancing your sales process, the Lead Form Step in Demo Hub is a powerful addition to your qualification toolkit.
