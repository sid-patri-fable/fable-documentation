# Overview of Demo Hub

Demo Hub is the perfect way to showcase all your interactive demos in a well-organized, visually appealing format. It allows you to categorize demos into sections, each with its own title and description, creating a structured catalog that highlights different features of your app. This makes it easy for prospects and visitors to explore and experience your app’s capabilities through Fable’s interactive demos.

Beyond simply showcasing demos, Demo Hub offers powerful qualification features. You can engage users by asking about their interests and preferences, and then dynamically display the most relevant demos based on their responses. The qualification builder lets you add single-select, multi-select options, lead forms, and text steps, making it easy to tailor the experience to your audience.

Check out this [Demo Hub](https://app.sharefable.com/hub/seeall/fable-ihxi2rpy9h0u4ivh) to see how it can elevate the way you present your app's features.
