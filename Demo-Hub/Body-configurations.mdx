# Configuring the Demo Hub Body

The body configuration settings in Fable's Demo Hub editor offer a comprehensive set of options to customize the appearance and functionality of your Demo Hub. These settings allow you to create a cohesive, branded experience that effectively showcases your demos. Here’s what you can configure:

### 1. **Body Configuration**
The main body of your Demo Hub is where the sections and demos are displayed. You can personalize this area by:

- **Introductory Text**: Add a welcoming or informative introductory text to guide your users as they explore your Demo Hub.
- **Background and Font Colors**: Customize the background color and font color to match your brand’s style, ensuring a visually appealing and consistent look throughout your Demo Hub.

### 2. **Demo Card Configuration**
Each demo is represented by a card within a section. The Demo Hub editor provides granular control over these cards, allowing you to tailor them to your liking:

- **Background, Border, and Font Colors**: Modify these elements to create a distinct and attractive appearance for each demo card.
- **Border Radius**: Adjust the border radius to give your demo cards a sharp or rounded look, depending on your design preferences.
- **CTA Customization**: The call-to-action (CTA) on each demo card prompts users to engage with the content. You can edit the CTA text, as well as customize its background, font color, and border radius to make it more inviting and aligned with your overall design.

### 3. **Demo Modal Configuration**
When a user clicks on a demo card, the demo opens in a modal window. You can enhance this modal by customizing:

- **Body and Overlay**: Adjust the background, border, and font colors of the modal, as well as the border radius to create a polished and professional look. You can also change the overlay color to set the right mood and focus attention on the demo content.

### 4. **Lead Form Configuration**
If your Demo Hub includes a lead form, you can configure its appearance and functionality:

- **Button Customization**: Modify the button's background, font color, text, and border radius to ensure it stands out and encourages user interaction.
- **Lead Form Options**: Decide whether users can skip the lead form and whether the lead form should be displayed. These options help you balance user experience with lead generation goals.

These configuration options give you the flexibility to design a Demo Hub that is both visually appealing and highly functional, enhancing the way your audience interacts with your demos.
