# Text Step Qualification

Text Step Qualification is a unique and informative feature within Demo Hub that allows you to seamlessly incorporate text-based content into the qualification process. Unlike other qualifications that involve selecting options, the Text Step is designed to provide users with valuable information, instructions, or context before they proceed further. This can be particularly useful for guiding users, offering product insights, or setting the stage for the demos they are about to explore.

With Text Step Qualification, you have the flexibility to display any relevant text content at a specific point in the qualification flow. For instance, if you're guiding users through different features of your application, you might want to introduce each section with a brief overview or important instructions. In Fable, where users might navigate through demos related to demo editing, adding integrations, or creating demo hubs, a Text Step could be used to explain what each feature entails or to highlight key benefits.

Consider the following example: As users begin their journey in the Demo Hub, you could insert a Text Step with a message like, "Welcome to Fable's Demo Hub! Before you start exploring, here’s a quick overview of what you can do: Edit and customize demos to fit your needs, integrate seamlessly with your favorite tools, and create comprehensive demo hubs to showcase your product. Select the features that interest you to see relevant demos."

This step serves as an introduction, ensuring that users understand the context and purpose of the demos they are about to see. It can also be used to provide instructions, such as, "Please make sure you have selected all the options that apply to your needs before proceeding," or to share important product updates or announcements.

Text Step Qualification is an excellent way to enhance the user experience by delivering crucial information at the right moment. It allows you to maintain control over the narrative, ensuring that users are well-informed and engaged throughout their interaction with the Demo Hub. Whether you’re providing product information, guiding users through the process, or setting expectations, the Text Step adds a layer of clarity and professionalism to your Demo Hub.

By incorporating Text Step Qualification, you can create a more structured and informative journey for your users, making their experience not only interactive but also educational. This approach helps to build a deeper understanding of your product and ensures that users are better prepared to engage with the demos that are most relevant to them.
