# Publishing a Demo Hub

After making updates to your Demo Hub, it's crucial to publish those changes so that your prospects can see the most up-to-date content. The process of publishing a Demo Hub is simple and intuitive. Once you've made your changes, you can easily preview them by clicking the "Preview" button in the header. This allows you to review the updates and ensure everything looks perfect.

If the preview meets your expectations, you can finalize the process by clicking the "Publish" button, also located in the header. This will make your Demo Hub live with the latest changes, ensuring your audience has access to the most relevant and polished demos.
