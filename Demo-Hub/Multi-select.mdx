# Multi-Select Qualification

The Multi-Select Qualification is a versatile and powerful feature within Demo Hub, designed to offer users a more customized and comprehensive experience. Unlike Single Select, where users choose just one option, Multi-Select allows them to pick multiple options that best reflect their interests. This enables the Demo Hub to present a broader range of demos, tailored to multiple aspects of their preferences.

With Multi-Select Qualification, you can present a series of options related to different features or sections of your application. For example, in Fable, users might be interested in various areas such as demo editing, adding integrations, creating demo hubs, and more. You could create a Multi-Select Qualification with a title like "Which features of Fable are you interested in?" and provide several options:
- A. Demo Editing
- B. Adding Integrations
- C. Demo Hubs
- D. Advanced Analytics

Users can then select any combination of these options, and the Demo Hub will generate a customized set of demos that align with all the selected interests. This flexibility allows users to explore multiple features simultaneously, ensuring they get a comprehensive view of the capabilities that matter most to them.

Multi-Select Qualification is particularly useful when prospects have diverse needs or when your application offers a wide range of features that can be showcased together. By allowing users to select multiple interests, you create a more engaging and informative experience, catering to the varied preferences of your audience.

Incorporating Multi-Select Qualification into your Demo Hub not only enhances the user journey but also strengthens the connection between your product and its potential users. By offering a personalized and expansive demo experience, you can effectively highlight the full breadth of your application's value, making a lasting impact on your prospects.
