# Editing the Header in Demo Hub

The header in your Demo Hub is a crucial component that sets the tone for your visitors' experience. It features your company logo on the left side, a title, a background color, and various call-to-action (CTA) buttons. Customizing these elements ensures that your Demo Hub reflects your brand's unique style and effectively engages your audience.

## Header Customization Options

1. **Company Logo**: The company logo is displayed prominently on the left-hand side of the header. You can upload your logo to maintain brand consistency and enhance recognition.

2. **Title**: Add or edit the title displayed in the header to clearly communicate the purpose of your Demo Hub or the specific section being showcased.

3. **Background Color**: Choose a background color that complements your branding and sets the right visual tone for the header, helping to create an inviting and cohesive look.

4. **CTAs**: Customize the call-to-action buttons included in the header. You can add, remove, or adjust the CTAs to guide users towards important actions, such as signing up, learning more, or contacting you.

The Demo Hub editor provides detailed controls for these settings, allowing you to fine-tune each element to align with your brand’s aesthetic. This level of customization ensures that your Demo Hub not only looks professional but also functions effectively to meet your objectives.
