Fable offers a versatile masking feature that allows you to apply image masks to specific elements on your demo screen, giving you greater control over the visual presentation. This feature is particularly valuable when you need to customize or update certain UI components, such as replacing default logos, icons, or placeholders with your own branded content. By doing so, you can ensure that your demo aligns seamlessly with your company's branding and delivers a polished, professional appearance.

This capability is ideal for situations where you want to tailor the look and feel of your demo to match your brand's identity or when showcasing a white-label solution to clients. For example, you can use image masks to update logos in real-time, giving your prospects a personalized experience that reflects their brand, or replace standard UI elements with unique, brand-specific designs. The masking feature enables you to enhance the visual coherence of your demo without needing to rework the entire layout, making it an efficient tool for customizing presentations.

Whether you're fine-tuning the aesthetics for a specific audience or ensuring brand consistency across demos, Fable’s image masking feature empowers you to deliver an engaging, on-brand experience every time.
