# Overview

Fable provides a range of powerful customization options to modify the screens in your interactive demos, allowing you to present your product exactly the way you want. These features give you the flexibility to tailor the content of each demo to suit your audience and message, ensuring a seamless and personalized experience. Here’s a breakdown of what you can do:

1. **Change Text Content**  
   You can easily modify the text on any screen, whether it's updating numbers, replacing paragraphs, or tweaking individual labels. This is particularly useful if you need to localize your demo for different regions or adjust messaging to fit a specific prospect’s use case. By keeping the content relevant and precise, you ensure that your demo speaks directly to your audience.

2. **Hide Elements**  
   If there are unnecessary or distracting elements on your screen, Fable allows you to hide them effortlessly. This feature comes in handy when you're presenting to different audiences with varying priorities— for instance, removing advanced options when targeting less technical viewers or simplifying the view to focus on core functionalities.

3. **Blur Elements**  
   Sometimes, you may need to conceal sensitive or proprietary information without removing it entirely from the screen. In such cases, you can blur elements to make them unreadable, protecting important details while still maintaining the overall layout. This feature is particularly helpful when sharing internal or confidential demos with external audiences.

4. **Mask Elements with Images**  
   Fable allows you to apply image masks to elements on the screen, which is ideal for customizing the appearance of specific UI components. For example, you can use this feature to replace logos, icons, or placeholders with branded content, ensuring that your demo aligns perfectly with your company’s visual identity.

5. **Replace Images**  
   If your demo contains outdated or irrelevant images, you can easily swap them out with new ones. This feature is great for keeping your demo fresh and up-to-date, whether you need to update product screenshots, include new branding materials, or personalize images for specific clients. By replacing images, you ensure that the visuals remain relevant and impactful.

These features enable you to fine-tune your interactive demos to make them as engaging, clear, and professional as possible. Explore the possibilities and create customized experiences that resonate with your audience, no matter the context.
