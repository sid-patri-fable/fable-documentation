# Replace image

Keeping your demo visually engaging and up-to-date is crucial, and Fable makes this simple by allowing you to easily replace any outdated or irrelevant images on your screens. Whether you're showcasing new product updates, refreshing branding elements, or tailoring visuals to suit a specific client’s needs, this feature enables you to swap out images seamlessly without disrupting the overall flow of your presentation.

This functionality is particularly beneficial when you need to maintain relevance across different demos. For example, if your product design has evolved or you're launching a new version, you can quickly update screenshots or feature visuals to reflect the latest changes. Similarly, when presenting to prospective clients, you can personalize the demo by incorporating their logos or industry-specific images, creating a more tailored and engaging experience.

By replacing images with fresh and relevant content, you ensure that your demo remains visually impactful and aligned with your current messaging. It also adds a layer of professionalism, as you can easily ensure that all visuals match the context of your presentation, leaving a strong impression on your audience.
