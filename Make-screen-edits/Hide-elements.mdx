Fable provides a simple yet powerful way to enhance the clarity of your demos by allowing you to hide unwanted or distracting elements on your screen. Whether you're presenting to a diverse audience or tailoring your demo for specific user groups, this feature ensures that your content remains focused and relevant to the viewer.

For example, when showcasing your product to non-technical users, you can easily remove complex or advanced options to create a more streamlined and accessible experience. By hiding unnecessary elements, you allow your audience to concentrate on the core features that matter most to them. Similarly, if your demo contains information that isn’t pertinent to a particular prospect or industry, you can quickly simplify the screen to highlight the essential functionality without overwhelming the viewer.

This feature is particularly valuable in dynamic presentations where different stakeholders may have varying levels of expertise or interests. By customizing what each audience sees, you ensure that your message is always clear, relevant, and impactful.
