# Blur elements

In many cases, you may need to display sensitive or proprietary information in your demo, but removing it entirely might disrupt the overall design or flow of the presentation. Fable offers a solution with its blur feature, allowing you to conceal specific elements on the screen by making them unreadable while still maintaining the structure and layout of your demo.

This feature is particularly useful when sharing internal or confidential demos with external audiences, such as prospective clients or partners. By blurring sensitive data, such as financial figures, private customer information, or unreleased features, you can protect your organization’s confidential details without compromising the clarity or professionalism of your demo. This ensures that viewers see only the information that’s relevant to them, while all critical or sensitive elements remain securely obscured.

Whether you're presenting to stakeholders, running a live demo, or sharing pre-recorded presentations, the blur feature provides peace of mind, allowing you to control exactly what gets revealed and what stays protected.
