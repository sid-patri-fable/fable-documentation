In Fable, you can add lead forms to capture details of the demo viewers.

## Adding a Lead Form

You can add a lead form to any guide within the demo. Fable has a plethora of pre-set options like Email, First Name, Last Name, Company etc. You can also add custom fields within the lead form. The following interactive demo shows how you can add a lead form to an interactive demo:
