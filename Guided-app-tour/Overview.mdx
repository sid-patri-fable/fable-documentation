## Overview of Guided App Tours in Fable

Fable is designed to offer a seamless and intuitive experience right from the start. To help you quickly get familiar with the app’s features, Fable includes a series of guided tours. These tours introduce you to the essentials, allowing you to navigate the platform with confidence and start creating stunning demos in no time.

### What to Expect from Guided Tours

When you first launch Fable, you'll encounter helpful cards in various sections of the app. These guided tours ensure you know where to find everything you need, making your experience intuitive and user-friendly.

### Example: Canvas Editor Guided Tour

One of the key guided tours appears when you open the Canvas Editor for the first time. This tour walks you through the editor's tools and features, helping you get started quickly. You can choose to take the tour immediately by clicking the primary CTA or skip it for later.

Another guided tour introduces you to the Demos page, highlighting its key features and functionalities. This tour ensures you understand how to manage, customize, and share your demos effectively.

### Why Guided Tours Matter

These tours are designed to reduce the learning curve and empower you to make the most of Fable. By following them, you’ll save time and quickly become proficient in creating high-quality demos that resonate with your audience.

### Conclusion

Fable’s guided tours are your first step towards mastering the platform. They provide a quick and effective way to familiarize yourself with the app, ensuring you can create compelling demos that captivate your audience and drive results.
