## Overview of Creating Demos Without Recording

When you use Fable's browser extension to record an interactive demo of your application, every captured screen is automatically saved in a dedicated space called the Screen Library. This library becomes a valuable resource, allowing you to build new demos without the need to re-record anything. The key advantage here is that these are fully interactive screens, not just static screenshots. This means you can leverage the full power of Fable's tools to create high-fidelity, dynamic demos. With all the features at your disposal, you can craft polished and engaging presentations that showcase your application in the best possible light.
