# Embedding a Demo

Once you’ve published the changes to your demo, the next step is to share it with your audience. Embedding your demo on a landing page is one of the most effective ways to showcase it, offering a seamless and interactive experience directly on your website. 

Fable makes this process straightforward by providing a ready-to-use code snippet that you can easily embed into your site. The snippet, formatted as an iframe, allows you to integrate the demo into your webpage with just a few lines of code. Here’s an example of what that might look like:

```html
<iframe 
  style="border: 1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1);" 
  width="100%" 
  height="100%" 
  src="https://app.sharefable.com/embed/demo/new-name-bxb5waa3i48l7uho" 
  allowfullscreen>
</iframe>
```

You can customize the size of the embedded demo by adjusting the `height` and `width` parameters in the iframe snippet, ensuring that it fits perfectly within your webpage’s design.

Embedding your demo not only enhances user engagement but also allows you to maintain control over how the demo is presented. The embedded demo integrates seamlessly into your content, creating a professional and cohesive look for your landing page.
