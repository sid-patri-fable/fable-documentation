# Comprehensive Guide to Demo-Sharing Features

Once your interactive demo is fully prepared, it's time to share it with your audience and prospects. Here's everything you need to know about sharing your demo effectively.

### Publishing Your Demo
When you create a demo and make changes to it, the updates must be published before your audience can view them. This step ensures that the latest version of your demo is accessible to your end users. If you modify an already published demo, those updates will also require publishing to reflect the changes.

### Sharing Options
Fable offers versatile options for sharing your demo. One option is embedding your demo directly into your website using a code snippet generated by Fable. This snippet provides a seamless way to integrate your demo into any webpage, offering a polished, interactive experience for your users.

In addition to embedding, you can also share your demo via a direct link. This allows you to distribute your demo across various platforms easily, ensuring that your prospects can access it wherever they are.

With Fable’s flexible sharing features, you can confidently showcase your demos in the most effective way possible, reaching your audience exactly where they are.
