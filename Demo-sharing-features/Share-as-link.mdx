# Sharing Your Demo as a Link

Fable provides a seamless way to share your interactive demo by generating a shareable link. When your demo is accessed through this link, it is presented in a personalized environment that you can fully customize to reflect your brand or message. 

With Fable, you can modify various elements of the demo page to enhance its visual appeal and coherence with your overall branding. Customize the header color, adjust the background gradient, and update the title and call-to-action (CTA) in the header to create a tailored experience for your audience. This level of personalization ensures that your demo stands out and resonates with your viewers.
