# Previewing Demo Changes

After making updates to your demo—whether it's editing annotation text, adding new annotations, or removing existing ones—it's essential to see how these changes will appear before publishing. Fable simplifies this process by allowing you to preview your demo in real-time, ensuring that every detail meets your expectations before going live.

Fable's intuitive preview feature lets you review your demo across multiple viewports, including mobile (both landscape and portrait orientation) and desktop screens of various sizes. This flexibility allows you to fine-tune your demo’s appearance, ensuring it looks perfect on any device your audience might use. By previewing your demo, you can catch and correct any issues, guaranteeing a polished and professional final product.
