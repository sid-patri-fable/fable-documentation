# Demo Conversion and Completion Rate

## Conversions

In Fable, most interactive demos feature a Call-to-Action (CTA), strategically placed either within the demo or, more commonly, at the end. These CTAs are crucial for driving the desired actions that align with your campaign goals. For example, you might include a CTA to book a demo with your sales team or to prompt users to sign up for a free trial. Tracking the effectiveness of these CTAs is essential for understanding how well your demo is converting viewers into active leads.

Fable automatically tracks these conversions and presents them as a percentage, showing you the conversion rate of your demo. This metric gives you a clear indication of how many viewers are taking the desired action after interacting with your demo.

## Completion Metrics

Every demo is composed of multiple steps, and not every viewer will make it to the end. Fable’s completion metrics provide insight into how much of your demo each prospect has viewed before dropping off. This data is presented in a histogram format, allowing you to see, for example, how many prospects watched 25% of your demo, how many made it to 50%, and so on.

These completion metrics are invaluable for identifying points where viewers lose interest or disengage. By analyzing where drop-offs occur, you can make targeted adjustments to the content or flow of your demo to enhance the overall completion rate, ensuring that more prospects are fully engaged from start to finish.
