# Demo Analytics Overview in Fable

Every Fable demo automatically captures a wealth of actionable analytics, enabling you to make data-driven business decisions. These insights are presented on beautifully designed dashboards in Fable, with no setup required!

Fable tracks two main types of analytics:
1. Demo Analytics
2. Lead Information

### Demo Analytics
Fable collects the following key data points:
1. Total sessions
2. Unique impressions
3. Session duration
4. Leads captured
5. Conversion rate
6. Demo completion rates
7. Session drop-off at each step

### Lead Information
For lead activity, Fable captures:
1. Lead journey through the demo
2. Lead form details
3. Total sessions per lead
4. Time spent
5. Completion percentage
6. CTA clicks
7. Device information
8. Location data

This comprehensive data provides valuable insights into buyer behavior, allowing you to tailor your demos and refine your go-to-market strategy.
