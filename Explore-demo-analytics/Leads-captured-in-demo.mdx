# Leads Captured in Demos

Capturing lead information through demos is crucial for understanding and engaging with your prospects. By incorporating lead forms into your demos, usually at the beginning, you can gather valuable data about your leads. This data might include basic details such as email addresses and names, or more specific information like job titles and roles within their organizations. In addition to this direct input, Fable provides a wealth of supplementary information, giving you a comprehensive view of lead interactions:

1. **Lead Journey:** Fable tracks the detailed path a lead takes through the demo. This includes the exact date and time the demo was accessed, the duration spent on each annotation, and which CTAs were clicked. All of this information is displayed in a clear, intuitive timeline, making it easy to follow a lead’s engagement with your demo.

2. **Total Sessions per Lead:** This metric indicates how many times a particular lead has viewed your demo. It provides insight into the lead’s level of interest and engagement.

3. **Time Spent:** Fable measures the total amount of time each lead spends interacting with the demo. This helps you understand how long leads are engaged and whether they are spending adequate time exploring your content.

4. **Completion Percentage:** This metric shows how much of the demo the prospect has completed. It helps you gauge how effectively your demo is capturing and retaining interest throughout its duration.

5. **CTA Clicks:** This tracks whether the lead has clicked on any Call-to-Action buttons within the demo. It provides insight into how well your CTAs are performing and whether they are driving the desired actions.

6. **Device Information:** Fable captures whether the demo was viewed on a desktop or mobile device. This information is useful for understanding the platforms your leads prefer and ensuring your demo is optimized for all devices.

7. **Location Data:** This includes detailed location information about the lead, such as their country, region, and city. Understanding where your leads are geographically located can help you tailor your marketing and sales strategies accordingly.
