# Demo Session Information

In Fable, a demo's session information within the analytics dashboard provides crucial insights into how your demo is performing. This data helps you understand viewer engagement and can be instrumental in optimizing your demos for better results. The session information includes the following key metrics:

1. **Total Sessions:** This metric indicates the total number of times your demo has been viewed. It gives you a clear picture of overall interest and reach.

2. **Unique Impressions:** Out of the total views, this metric tells you how many of those were unique visitors. It helps you gauge the actual number of individual prospects engaging with your demo, filtering out repeat views from the same user.

3. **Session Time:** This metric tracks how much time prospects spend on your demo. It is presented as a histogram, allowing you to visualize the distribution of session lengths. Additionally, you can see the median time spent on the demo, offering insights into the average engagement duration across your audience.

Understanding these metrics is vital for assessing the effectiveness of your demos and identifying areas for improvement. By analyzing session data, you can refine your content to better meet the needs and expectations of your prospects, ultimately driving higher conversions.
