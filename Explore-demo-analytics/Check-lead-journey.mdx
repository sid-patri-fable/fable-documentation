# Checking a Lead's Journey

In Fable, understanding a lead's interaction with your demo is essential, especially since a lead might view your demo multiple times. Fable provides a detailed and granular view of each lead's journey through the demo, offering valuable insights into their engagement patterns and behavior. Here’s how you can explore this information:

1. **Access Times and Dates:** Fable records the exact date and time each lead opens your demo. This detailed timestamp data helps you analyze when leads are most likely to engage with your content and can uncover trends related to time of day or frequency of access.

2. **Time Spent on Annotations:** Each lead's interaction with individual annotations is meticulously tracked. You can see how long they spend on each annotation, which allows you to identify which sections of your demo attract the most interest and which may need enhancements to retain viewer attention.

3. **CTA Clicks:** Fable tracks interactions with Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons within your demo. This data shows whether leads are engaging with CTAs, which can be pivotal for assessing the effectiveness of your calls-to-action and whether they are compelling enough to drive leads toward the desired actions.

4. **Total Session Time:** The total amount of time a lead spends on the demo is recorded. This metric gives you an overall view of engagement and helps you understand how thoroughly leads are exploring your demo content. It’s useful for evaluating the depth of engagement and identifying any potential areas where leads might lose interest.

5. **Session History:** You can view a complete history of each lead’s interactions, including repeated visits and varying engagement levels. This comprehensive view helps you understand how leads interact with your demo over time and identify any changes in their behavior.

The Fable demo embedded below provides a visual guide on how to access and interpret these detailed metrics. By examining this information, you can gain deeper insights into each lead's journey, refine your demo content, and optimize engagement strategies for better conversion outcomes.
