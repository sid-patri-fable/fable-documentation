### How to Add a New Screen in Fable

Fable makes it easy to add new screens to your demo, allowing you to create dynamic, multi-step experiences that guide your users through different parts of your application. You have two primary ways to add new screens, giving you the flexibility to customize your demo exactly as needed.

1. **Reuse Existing Screens**: Fable enables you to reuse screens that you’ve previously captured. This feature is incredibly useful if you need to reference or highlight the same interface at multiple stages of your demo. By recycling screens, you can save time and maintain consistency across different parts of the presentation, ensuring your users remain focused on the key areas of your app.

2. **Upload a New Screen**: If you need to introduce a completely new screen that hasn’t been captured before, Fable allows you to upload images directly. Whether you want to showcase an entirely different section of your app, update the visual content with new designs, or simply add a new view to your demo, uploading an image is quick and easy. This option ensures that your demo remains fresh and adaptable, even as your application evolves.

Adding new screens to your demo is a great way to enrich the user experience and walk prospects through different scenarios or workflows within your app. You can create a cohesive, multi-screen journey that illustrates the full range of your app’s capabilities, ensuring your users see the full picture.

For a step-by-step guide on how to add both existing and new screens to your demo, check out the Fable demo below. It demonstrates the process and highlights how easy it is to incorporate new screens into your interactive presentations.
