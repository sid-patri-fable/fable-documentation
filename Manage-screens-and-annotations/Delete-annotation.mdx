### How to Delete an Annotation in Fable

Fable provides a simple and flexible way to delete annotations, ensuring you can easily remove any annotations that are no longer needed or were added by mistake. You have two convenient methods for deleting an annotation, both designed to fit seamlessly into your workflow.

1. **Delete from the Canvas Editor**: The first method allows you to delete the annotation directly from the canvas editor. In this view, you can visually inspect and interact with the annotations as they appear on the screen. Simply select the annotation you want to remove, and with a quick action, you can delete it right from the canvas. This method is especially helpful when you're actively working on or reviewing your annotations in the visual interface.

2. **Delete from the Creator Panel**: The second method involves deleting annotations from the Creator Panel. This panel gives you an organized view of your annotations, making it easy to find and delete specific ones, even if you're working with a large number of annotations in your demo. The Creator Panel method is useful when you need to manage your annotations in bulk or when navigating through the layers of annotations in your project.

Both methods offer flexibility depending on how you're interacting with your annotations—whether through a hands-on visual approach in the canvas or through an organized list in the Creator Panel.

To see these options in action, check out the Fable demo below, which walks you through the process of deleting annotations using both methods, ensuring you have complete control over your annotation management.
