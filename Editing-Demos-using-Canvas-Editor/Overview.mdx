### Overview of Canvas

1. **Annotation Cards**: The canvas includes a sequence of annotation cards, which can be accessed by clicking on them. Each card allows you to view and modify the annotation content, offering a streamlined way to manage your notes and comments within the canvas.

2. **Canvas Tools**: Positioned on the left side of the canvas, the tools available here are essential for customizing and enhancing your canvas experience. They allow you to add custom loaders, incorporate new modules, add additional screens, and ensure mobile responsiveness, among other functionalities. These tools help you tailor the demo to your specific needs.

3. **Header Options**: The header of the canvas provides a range of options for managing your demos. From here, you can preview your work, publish updates, share content with others, and access detailed analytics. These options are designed to streamline your workflow and provide comprehensive control over your demos.
