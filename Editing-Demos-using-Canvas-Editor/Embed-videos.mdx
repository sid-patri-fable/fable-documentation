### Embedding Videos in Annotations

Fable offers a seamless way to enhance your annotations by embedding videos directly into your content. With just a few simple steps, you can enrich your annotations with engaging video content. 

To embed a video, simply copy and paste the URL of your favorite video from YouTube or Vimeo into the annotation content area. Fable automatically converts this link into an embedded video player, allowing your viewers to watch the video directly within the annotation.

This feature makes it easy to provide additional context, instructions, or examples directly within your annotations, enhancing the overall user experience. The demo below illustrates how straightforward and effective embedding videos in annotations can be.
