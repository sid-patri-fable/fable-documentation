### Editing Annotation Text

Fable's rich text editor offers a robust set of features for customizing and enhancing your text annotations. With this editor, you can:

- **Format Text**: Easily apply bold and italic styles to emphasize key points or add a touch of creativity to your annotations.
- **Adjust Font Size and Alignment**: Tailor the appearance of your text by adjusting the font size and aligning it according to your preferences, whether it’s left, center, or right.
- **Incorporate Images**: Enrich your annotations by embedding images directly within the text, providing a visual context to your content.
- **Add Hyperlinks**: Insert hyperlinks to other documents, web pages, or resources, allowing readers to access additional information effortlessly.
- **Include Lead Forms**: Enhance interactivity by integrating lead forms, enabling you to capture valuable user information directly within your annotations.

The Fable demo provided below showcases these features in action, giving you a clear view of how each functionality can be utilized to create dynamic and informative text annotations.
