# How to Integrate with HubSpot

HubSpot is one of the most powerful CRM tools available, and Fable integrates seamlessly with it to capture your lead data efficiently.

The demo below walks you through the HubSpot integration process.

<FableEmbed src="https://app.sharefable.com/embed/demo/hubspot-integration-7dbg97m3y0izclkb" />

Here’s a step-by-step guide:

### Step 1: Access the Integrations Tab
Navigate to the Integrations tab in the Fable app's sidebar. Scroll through the list to find HubSpot, then click on it. A modal will appear with a "Connect" button.

<img alt="HubSpot Integration" src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/619f416a-7353-45c6-82ff-29b0c157aa80"></img>

### Step 2: Connect to HubSpot
Clicking the "Connect" button will redirect you to HubSpot in a new tab, where you’ll see a list of your HubSpot accounts.

<img alt="HubSpot Account Selection" src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/4083938d-a922-4688-b89f-9e5e347fc20f"></img>

Select your account and proceed.

### Step 3: Grant Permissions
You’ll be presented with a page listing the access permissions that Fable is requesting from HubSpot. Simply click on "Connect App."

<img alt="HubSpot Permissions" src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/2c0aae93-f5de-4c07-a368-b2a5d7182a94"></img>

Once connected, HubSpot will display a success screen confirming the integration.

<img alt="HubSpot Success Screen" src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/627b740a-af6b-4996-bc90-c08b178a9cf8"></img>

### Step 4: Verify the Integration
Return to the Fable screen to confirm that HubSpot has been successfully integrated.

<img alt="HubSpot Integration Success" src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/11166525-ec8a-4032-982e-764e0983bead"></img>

Now, all lead information captured by Fable demos will be automatically synced with your HubSpot account.

And that's how easy it is to integrate HubSpot with Fable!
