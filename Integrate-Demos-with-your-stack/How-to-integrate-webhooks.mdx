# How to Integrate Webhooks

Webhooks enable real-time data updates between your systems, enhancing the efficiency and responsiveness of your sales and marketing processes.

Connecting webhooks with Fable is straightforward! The demo below guides you through the webhook integration process.

<FableEmbed src="https://app.sharefable.com/embed/demo/webhook-integration-wwvxqmirws4f12me" />

Here’s a step-by-step guide:

### Step 1: Access the Integrations Tab
In the Fable app's sidebar, go to the Integrations tab. Scroll to find "Webhook" and click on it. A modal will appear with a "Create a new webhook" button.

<img alt="Integration Tab" src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/ab13bb2f-e8b4-44c4-bff7-f51a9612c580"></img>

### Step 2: Add the Webhook URL
Enter the webhook URL in the first input field. This URL is where the lead information from your demo will be sent.

<img alt="Webhook URL Input" src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/f2e5abf6-4b6e-496e-97e0-9e600cc66eec"></img>

### Step 3: Define the Request Body
Customize the structure of the request body. Fable provides a default structure that includes the first name, last name, email, and demo URL. You can modify this structure to fit your needs.

### Step 4: Add Request Headers
If your webhook requires authentication, you can add the necessary request headers, such as an auth bearer token. You can add as many headers as your use case requires.

Click "Save," and you're done!

That’s how you can integrate Webhooks with Fable in just four simple steps.
