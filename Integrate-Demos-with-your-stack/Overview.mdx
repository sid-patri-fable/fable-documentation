# Overview of Integrating Demos with your stack

With Fable’s integrations, you can seamlessly send demo events to your CRM, marketing automation tools, and more.

Once you've created a Fable demo and started tracking events, the next step is to set up your preferred integrations to capture this data.

Currently, Fable integrates with the following apps:

- Zapier
- Slack
- Pipedrive
- Webhooks
- Hubspot
- Salesforce
- Pardot
- Mailchimp
- Outreach

We are constantly working on bringing new integrations to you!
