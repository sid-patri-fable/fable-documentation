# How to Fix Overflowing Annotations

At times, annotation cards in your interactive demo may overflow beyond the viewport, leading to a cluttered and unpolished appearance. More importantly, when this happens, key information within the annotation card may be lost, compromising the user experience.

Several factors can cause annotation cards to overflow:

1. **Excessive Text Content**: If an annotation contains too much information and there isn't enough space to accommodate it, the card may spill outside the visible area.
2. **Large Font Sizes**: Using large fonts increases the space each line of text occupies, raising the likelihood of the annotation card overflowing.
3. **Narrow Width**: When the width of the annotation card is too narrow, it forces the content to stack vertically, making the card taller and potentially pushing parts of it out of view.

### Solutions to Prevent Overflowing Annotations:

1. **Keep Text Content Concise**: Aim to keep annotations short, clear, and to the point. Not only does this prevent overflow issues, but it also enhances readability and creates a more engaging experience for users.
2. **Use Appropriate Font Sizes**: Ensure the font size is suitable for the space available. Large fonts can be visually appealing, but they should be used with caution to avoid layout disruptions.
3. **Increase the Width of the Annotation Card**: If your annotation is becoming too tall and overflowing, consider increasing its width. A wider card will help balance the content and prevent excessive height that leads to overflow.

By following these guidelines, you can create annotations that are both visually appealing and functional, ensuring that important information is always accessible to your audience.
