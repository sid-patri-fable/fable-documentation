# Annotation Position Customization

Fable intelligently positions annotation cards by default, optimizing their placement based on the available screen space. This ensures that your annotations never block important content, creating a seamless user experience. However, if you prefer more control over where the annotations appear, Fable offers robust customization options that allow you to manually adjust their positioning.

With Fable's position customization feature, you can precisely place annotation cards around an element, choosing to position them at the top, bottom, left, or right. This flexibility ensures that the annotations fit perfectly within your layout and remain visually unobtrusive. Whether you're working with dynamic layouts or want to create a specific look for your interactive demos, Fable gives you the tools to align annotations exactly where they need to be.
