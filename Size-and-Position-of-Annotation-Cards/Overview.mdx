# Overview of Sizing and Positioning of Annotation Cards

Annotation cards in Fable's interactive demos are seamlessly attached to specific elements, providing helpful context and guidance. By default, Fable automatically optimizes the positioning of these cards based on the available screen space, ensuring they don't obstruct key content. However, Fable also offers full customization options, allowing you to take control of both the size and placement of these annotation cards.

You can easily adjust the card's width using preset sizes—small, medium, or large—or specify a custom width to fit your needs. Additionally, you have the flexibility to position the annotation cards precisely where you want, whether at the top, bottom, left, or right of an element. This ensures that your demos remain both intuitive and visually polished while giving you the freedom to personalize the experience to your audience's needs.
