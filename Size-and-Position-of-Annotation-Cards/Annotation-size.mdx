# Annotation Size Customization

Fable allows you to fully customize the size of annotation cards, offering flexibility to match your content and design preferences. You can choose from convenient preset sizes—small, medium, and large—which cater to most standard use cases. These preset options make it easy to quickly adapt the annotations to different scenarios without much effort.

For more specific needs, Fable also supports custom sizing. You can define the exact size of an annotation card by entering a value in pixels, ensuring a perfect fit for the content. This is particularly useful when working with varying amounts of text or visual elements, allowing the annotation to remain clear and legible without crowding or unnecessary white space.

The ability to adjust the size ensures your annotations maintain an optimal appearance and functionality across different screen sizes and content types.
