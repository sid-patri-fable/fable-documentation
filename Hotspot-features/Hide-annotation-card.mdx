### Hiding the Annotation Card

Fable enhances the interactivity of your demos with its innovative hotspot feature, which allows you to hide the annotation card. This functionality provides a more immersive and authentic experience for your users, mimicking the feel of using the application directly.

Here’s how it works: Instead of progressing through the demo by clicking the "Next" button on the annotation card, your prospects will interact directly with the annotated elements on the screen. By clicking on these elements, they can move forward in the demo, as if they are navigating the app independently.

This approach creates a more engaging and realistic demo experience. It helps users feel more connected to the application and better understand its features and functionality, as they are not distracted by navigation buttons or additional UI elements.

To see this feature in action, check out the Fable demo below. It illustrates how you can use the hotspot feature to hide the annotation card and provide a seamless, self-guided demo experience.
