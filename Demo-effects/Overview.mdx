# Demo Effects

Demo effects are a versatile set of visual enhancements that can be applied to annotations or entire screens within your Fable Demo. These effects not only elevate the visual appeal of your presentation but also enhance its functionality. By adding demo effects, you can emphasize key points, guide viewers' attention, and create a more engaging and dynamic experience that truly makes your demo stand out.
