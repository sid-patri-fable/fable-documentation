# Annotation Demo Effects

Fable provides a rich selection of annotation effects that can add dynamic visual elements to your demos, making them more engaging and visually striking. These effects allow you to highlight annotations in unique ways, ensuring your key points capture attention. Here’s an overview of the annotation demo effects available:

- **Wave**: The Wave effect adds a flowing, wave-like animation to your annotation, creating a sense of movement that draws the viewer’s eye and adds a dynamic flair to your demo.

  ![Wave Effect](https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/39b0bd0e-a5a0-4270-bf89-d52e9994ff4a)

- **Beckham**: With the Beckham effect, a subtle light bend is applied around the edge of your annotation, infusing it with a gentle glow that enhances its visibility and adds a polished finish.

  ![Beckham Effect](https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/3b860d1c-9869-4c63-bfd5-70ea6b82137e)

- **Bleeding Edge**: The Bleeding Edge effect creates a striking contrast by bleeding an opposite color from the edges of your annotation, making it stand out sharply against the background.

  ![Bleeding Edge Effect](https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/5dd471f4-9201-4ee0-8a4a-bc07eff37cd1)

- **Glass Ceiling**: The Glass Ceiling effect gives your annotation a sleek, glass-like appearance, adding a touch of sophistication and modernity to your demo. This effect mimics the look of frosted glass, blending clarity with style.

  ![Glass Ceiling Effect](https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/e8380754-8c89-4ddb-8183-76aefc0f5b9f)

- **Custom**: For those who want complete creative freedom, the Custom effect allows you to design your own annotation effects using CSS3, tailoring the look and feel to perfectly match your demo’s theme and tone.

These annotation effects can be seamlessly integrated into your Fable demos, enhancing the overall user experience by adding layers of visual interest and clarity.
