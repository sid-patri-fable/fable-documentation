# Screen Demo Effects

Fable offers a diverse range of screen effects that allow you to highlight elements and create a visually captivating experience in your demos. These effects are designed to focus attention, enhance the visual narrative, and bring a unique style to your presentation. Here’s a closer look at the available screen demo effects:

- **Sincity**: This effect transforms the entire screen to black-and-white, leaving only the selected element in color. It’s perfect for drawing sharp contrast and ensuring your key message stands out.

  ![Sincity Effect](https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/cdf185ac-b2b6-4953-83e7-c16cd1410006)

- **Clarity**: With Clarity, the entire page becomes blurred, except for the selected element. This effect subtly yet effectively guides the viewer’s focus to the most important part of your screen.

  ![Clarity Effect](https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/bae9de71-4cb7-437e-97a0-e238be0a8fbc)

- **Material Design**: Inspired by Google’s Material Design, this effect brings a clean and modern focus style to your demo. It’s ideal for presentations that require a professional and sleek appearance.

  ![Material Design Effect](https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/80c22ebf-cdbf-475c-9871-b513def96c2a)

- **Cyberpunk**: Dive into a futuristic, post-apocalyptic world with the Cyberpunk effect. This dramatic style brings bold colors and intense visuals to your demo, making it stand out with a unique edge.

  ![Cyberpunk Effect](https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/5c610acd-4b85-458e-8b76-0a488720a01c)

- **Color Swap**: The Color Swap effect allows you to change the background color of your demo, adding a fresh layer of customization to match your brand or theme.

  ![Color Swap Effect](https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/5ef4ffc3-4b41-4422-8a5f-6978e7210c72)

- **Custom**: For those who want to create something entirely unique, the Custom effect lets you write your own CSS3 code, giving you complete control over the appearance and behavior of your demo.
