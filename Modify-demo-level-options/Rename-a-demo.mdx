# Renaming a Demo

Each demo you create in Fable is identified by its unique name, which serves as its primary identifier. Renaming a demo is a simple process, allowing you to easily update the title to better reflect its content or purpose.

In the demo embedded below, you’ll find a step-by-step guide on how to rename a demo within the Fable app. This short tutorial walks you through the process, ensuring that you can effortlessly update the name of your demo whenever needed. Whether you’re fine-tuning your demos or aligning them with a new project, renaming is quick and straightforward.
