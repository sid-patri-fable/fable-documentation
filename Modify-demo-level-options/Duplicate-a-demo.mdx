# Duplicating a Demo

Fable makes it easy to duplicate a demo, allowing you to create a copy of an existing demo with just a few clicks. This feature is particularly handy when you need to make slight adjustments or tailor a demo for different audiences or scenarios without starting from scratch. By duplicating a demo, you save valuable time and effort, enabling you to quickly iterate on your work or create variations for different purposes.

The tutorial below will walk you through the simple steps required to duplicate a demo within Fable, ensuring that you can efficiently manage and modify your demos as needed.
