# Overview of Demo-Level Options

When you log into the Fable app, you’ll be greeted with the Demos screen, which serves as your home base. Here, you can easily access and manage all the demos you've created.

For first-time users, the experience is designed to be intuitive and helpful. If you haven’t created any demos yet, you’ll be presented with a selection of sample demos. These samples are thoughtfully crafted to showcase what an interactive demo should look and feel like, offering inspiration and guidance as you create your own. Alongside these sample demos, you’ll find clear instructions on the next steps to complete your onboarding process. This includes installing the browser extension (if you haven’t already), creating your first demo, and eventually sharing it or embedding it on your website.

The Demos screen is the central hub where you can manage your interactive demos, whether you’re just starting out or looking to refine your creations.
