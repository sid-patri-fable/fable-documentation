# How to Create an Account on Fable

Creating an account with Fable is a quick and straightforward process. Follow these simple steps to get started:

1. **Navigate to the Login Page**
   Head over to [sharefable.com](https://sharefable.com) and click the **Login** button located in the top right corner of the header.
   <img alt="..." src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/51c89540-9d15-4cbf-90e1-b658f3ecbb6f"></img>

2. **Sign Up**
   You'll be directed to the signup page where you can create an account using your email and password, or conveniently sign up with your Google account.
   <img alt="..." src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/86f1d13a-87fd-4b7e-a184-cded91d4b43f"></img>

3. **Complete Your Profile**
   After successfully signing up, you'll enter the onboarding flow. Here, you'll be prompted to fill in your first and last name to complete your profile.
   <img alt="..." src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/a2a5c607-1f9f-44c6-8596-0ce10540fda0"></img>

4. **Create Your Organization**
   Next, you'll need to create an organization. This step helps organize your team's workspaces within Fable.
   <img alt="..." src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/4d873708-ac62-49ce-8479-41062508b25a"></img>

5. **Select Your Use Cases**
   You'll then select the use cases that best describe how you plan to use Fable. This ensures your experience is tailored to your needs.
   <img alt="..." src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/bfbf4ec9-9c5f-4f86-a92b-6dc9e17eb1f9"></img>

Once you've completed these steps, you'll be taken directly into the app, and you're all set to start using Fable! In just a few minutes, you can create an account and begin exploring all the features Fable has to offer. 
   <img alt="..." src="https://documentden-deployments.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/public/76743294-6a75-47ad-be1c-36c32baf7d06"></img>
