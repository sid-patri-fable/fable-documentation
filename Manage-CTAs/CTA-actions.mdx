In Fable, CTAs (Call-to-Action buttons) are featured prominently on annotation cards, which typically include default buttons for navigation—specifically, "Next" and "Back." However, Fable offers greater flexibility, allowing you to enhance these annotation cards with additional CTAs to better meet your needs, such as adding buttons for signing up, booking a demo, or other important actions.

Here are the actions you can take with CTAs in Fable:

1. **Delete**: Remove a CTA that is no longer needed or relevant.
2. **Hide**: Temporarily conceal a CTA from view without permanently deleting it.
3. **Edit**: Modify the text, link, or functionality of an existing CTA to better align with your goals.
4. **Add New CTA**: Introduce additional buttons to the annotation card, providing more options for user interaction.
5. **Change CTA Layout**: Adjust the arrangement and design of CTAs to fit your desired aesthetic and user experience.
