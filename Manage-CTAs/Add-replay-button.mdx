In addition to integrating CTAs for booking demos or accessing signup pages, Fable offers the option to configure a CTA as a "Replay" button. This feature is particularly useful for enhancing user engagement. For instance, at the end of your demo, you can set up a CTA that allows users to easily replay the entire demonstration with a single click, ensuring they have all the information they need or want to revisit specific parts of the demo.

This capability provides a seamless way to reinforce your message or provide additional value without requiring users to navigate away or initiate a new session manually.

To see how to implement this feature and explore the full range of CTA customization options, refer to the Fable demo below, which provides a step-by-step guide on setting up and configuring your CTAs effectively.
