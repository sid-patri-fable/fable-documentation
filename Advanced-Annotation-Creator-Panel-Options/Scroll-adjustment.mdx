# Scroll Adjustment Setting

The **Scroll Adjustment** setting in Fable gives you control over how annotated elements are scrolled into view during a demo, ensuring an optimal viewing experience for users. You can choose from three distinct options in the dropdown menu, each designed to handle scrolling behavior differently:

- **Auto**: In this mode, Fable automatically determines whether or not to scroll the annotated element into the center of the screen. It makes this decision based on available screen space and other contextual factors, providing a seamless, hands-off experience for most users.
  
- **Scroll**: When this option is selected, Fable will always scroll the annotated element to the center of the screen, guaranteeing that it remains fully visible, regardless of its initial position. This ensures that users can easily view and engage with the content without any manual adjustment.

- **Sticky**: This option disables automatic scrolling, keeping the annotated element in its current position on the page. It is useful when you want the demo to maintain a fixed layout or when the content is already in view.

While these options provide flexibility depending on your needs, the **Auto** setting is generally the best choice for most cases, as it dynamically adjusts scrolling based on the context of your demo, creating a more fluid user experience.
