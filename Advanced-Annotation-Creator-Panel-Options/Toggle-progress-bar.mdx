# Toggle Progress Bar

The **Progress Bar** feature gives you control over whether to display a progress bar at the top of your interactive demo. When enabled, this progress bar provides a clear visual indicator of the user's journey through the demo steps, showing how far they've progressed and how many steps remain.

This simple yet effective feature can greatly enhance user engagement, as it offers a sense of direction and accomplishment. Users can easily track their progress, which keeps them motivated to continue through the demo. By providing this visual cue, you improve the overall experience by reducing uncertainty about the demo's length and encouraging users to stay engaged until the end.

The Fable demo below demonstrates how to activate and customize the progress bar for your demo, helping you create a more user-friendly and engaging presentation.
