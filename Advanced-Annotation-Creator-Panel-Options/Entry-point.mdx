# Entry Point

The entry point in Fable refers to the specific annotation where your interactive demo will begin. Setting an entry point ensures that your audience is guided to the right starting point of your demo, establishing a smooth and logical flow from the first interaction onward.

To mark an annotation as the entry point, simply open the annotation in the Creator panel, navigate to the advanced settings, and check the "Entry Point" option. Once an annotation is designated as the entry point, a curved arrow will appear above the annotation card on the canvas, visually marking it as the starting point.

Setting the correct entry point is crucial for maintaining the structure and coherence of your demo, as it ensures that users are introduced to the demo at the right moment, providing a clear and engaging introduction.
