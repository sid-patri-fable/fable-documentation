# Reduce Motion on Mobile

The **Reduce Motion on Mobile** option is tailored to enhance the viewing experience for users on mobile devices, especially those with lower-powered hardware. By enabling this setting, Fable minimizes motion effects and animations, which can help improve performance and ensure a smoother, more responsive experience on mobile devices.

This feature is particularly beneficial if your target audience predominantly accesses your demo via mobile devices, as it helps to prevent potential performance issues and ensures that the demo remains visually appealing and functional. Reducing excessive motion can also make your demo more accessible to users who may be sensitive to animations or motion.

The Fable demo below provides a step-by-step guide on how to enable and configure the **Reduce Motion on Mobile** setting, allowing you to optimize your demo for a better user experience across various devices.
